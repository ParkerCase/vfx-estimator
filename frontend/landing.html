/ The Process

From breakdown to bid, in three moves.

01

Input

Ingest

Drop in a shot list, a script breakdown or a rough cut. FrameBid parses it the way a seasoned VFX supervisor would — identifying every shot, every asset build and hidden complexity.

02

Intelligence

Estimate

Every shot and every asset returns a defensible number: compositing, roto, FX, lighting, asset builds and render hours — benchmarked against thousands of comparable jobs so nothing slips through.

03

Output

Win

Export a studio-ready bid in minutes, complete with assumptions and margins. Quote faster, protect your profit and win the work before the competition finishes reading the script.
