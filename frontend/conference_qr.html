How it works

From script to bid in three steps

1

Paste your shot list or script

Drop in a scene breakdown, shot list, or even raw script pages. FrameBid parses it and creates a row per VFX shot automatically.

2

Set your project context

Choose shot type, complexity, scale, CG ratio, and your studio's day rates. FrameBid uses this as a lens when generating every estimate.

3

Review, adjust, and lock

AI estimates populate in seconds. Override any department day manually, add or remove assets, lock finalized shots, and export the completed bid.
